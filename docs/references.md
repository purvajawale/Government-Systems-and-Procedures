# Reference sources

Use the following official and public sources to verify current government procedures, rules, schemes, and financial processes.

## General government administration
- Government of India, Department of Personnel & Training (DoPT): https://dopt.gov.in/
- Central Secretariat Manual of Office Procedure (CSMOP): https://darpg.gov.in/
- Government of India e-Office and digital workflow resources: https://www.meity.gov.in/

## Budget and financial management
- Ministry of Finance, Government of India: https://www.finmin.nic.in/
- Union Budget and Expenditure: https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/
- Controller General of Accounts (CGA): https://cga.nic.in/

## Schemes and public delivery
- National Portal of India: https://www.india.gov.in/
- PM-KISAN / welfare schemes portals as applicable to the department being studied
- State government portal for department-specific schemes and programs

## Legal and procedural references
- Official Gazette of India: https://egazette.nic.in/
- Ministry/department websites for specific Acts, rules, notifications, and circulars
- State government gazettes and departmental orders for state-specific matters

## Guidance
Always cross-check the latest office order, scheme guideline, budget note, or departmental circular before relying on any example in this project.
